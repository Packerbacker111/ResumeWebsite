A hands-on web challenge

Who gets to decide?

There’s a flag behind this gate. The server says you can’t see it. Find the weakness in how it makes that decision.

01 / Mission

Turn access denied into access granted.

The gate trusts something it probably shouldn’t. Figure out what’s making the decision—and who really controls it.

Need a nudge?

A request carries more than a URL. Look at what travels between your browser and the server when you check the gate.

One more hint

Inspect the site’s cookies in your browser’s Application or Storage tools. Pay attention to the state scoped to /api/cookie-challenge. Can a value you control influence the server’s decision?
